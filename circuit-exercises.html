参考方向与吸收功率Reference directions and absorbed power

题目原文Transcribed question

已知在题图1-3所示电压、电流参考方向下，元件A的电压、电流分别为u=-3 V、i=5 A，元件B的电压、电流分别为u=5 V、i=-2 mA，试求两元件吸收的功率。Under the voltage and current reference directions shown in Fig. 1-3, element A has u = -3 V and i = 5 A, while element B has u = 5 V and i = -2 mA. Find the power absorbed by each element.

A：电流参考方向由左向右，电压左正右负；B：电流参考方向由右向左，电压右正左负。两者电流箭头都流入电压正参考端。A: current points left to right, with voltage positive on the left. B: current points right to left, with voltage positive on the right. Both current references enter the positive voltage-reference terminal.

先想：怎么识别与起步Hint: recognition and first step

前置知识Prerequisites

电压和电流参考方向；关联参考方向；功率符号与单位换算。Voltage and current reference directions; passive sign convention; power signs and unit conversion.
先看箭头是否流入标“+”的端子，再把带符号的数值代入公式。First check whether the current arrow enters the terminal marked +, then substitute the signed values.
完整解析与结果核验Full solution & verification

确认参考方向Check the reference directions

A和B的电流参考方向都流入电压正参考端，因此两者吸收功率公式均为p=ui。For both A and B, the current reference enters the positive voltage-reference terminal, so absorbed power is p = ui.

pabs=ui

计算ACalculate A

A的电压为负，按给定参考方向直接代入。负吸收功率表示该元件实际提供功率。Substitute the signed voltage for A. A negative absorbed power means that the element delivers power.

pA=(−3)(5)=−15W

计算B并统一单位Calculate B and convert units

把-2 mA换成-0.002 A。B的吸收功率为-0.010 W，即-10 mW。Convert -2 mA to -0.002 A. B absorbs -0.010 W, or -10 mW.

pB=5(−2×10−3)=−0.010W=−10mW

A吸收-15 W（提供15 W）；B吸收-10 mW（提供10 mW）。A absorbs -15 W (delivers 15 W); B absorbs -10 mW (delivers 10 mW).

易错点Common errors

  • 不要因i为负而翻转箭头后仍代入原负值，避免重复改符号。Do not reverse the arrow for a negative current and then keep the original negative value; that would change the sign twice.
  • mA与A相差1000倍。mA and A differ by a factor of 1000.

核验依据Verification

按关联参考方向独立计算，并与书第332页（PDF341页）1-5参考答案逐项一致。Independently calculated using the passive sign convention and matched item by item to answer 1-5 on printed p. 332 (PDF p. 341).

已知支路电流，结合KCL与KVL求未知电流Use KCL and KVL with known branch currents

题目原文Transcribed question

题图1-5所示电路为某电路的一部分，已知i₁=6 A，i₂=-2 A，求图中电流i。The circuit in Fig. 1-5 is part of a larger circuit. Given i₁ = 6 A and i₂ = -2 A, find the current i shown in the figure.

三个左端口分别经3 Ω电阻接入上、中、下节点，i₁、i₂、i₃参考方向均向右。上、中节点间6 Ω，中、下节点间18 Ω，上、下节点间右支路12 Ω；待求i沿12 Ω向下。Three left ports connect through 3 Ω resistors to the upper, middle, and lower nodes; i₁, i₂, and i₃ all point right. There are 6 Ω between upper and middle nodes, 18 Ω between middle and lower nodes, and 12 Ω in the right branch from upper to lower, with unknown i pointing downward.

先想：怎么识别与起步Hint: recognition and first step

前置知识Prerequisites

KCL、KVL、电阻关联参考方向。KCL, KVL, and the resistor passive sign convention.
已有两个注入电流，先用KCL把内部支路电流都写成i，再对两条上到下的路径列一次KVL；不用求三个左端口的电压。With two injection currents already known, use KCL to express the internal branch currents in terms of i, then equate the voltage drops along the two upper-to-lower paths. The left-port voltages are unnecessary.
完整解析与结果核验Full solution & verification

上节点列KCLApply KCL at the upper node

设6 Ω中的电流向下为i₆。流入的6 A分成i和i₆，所以i₆=6-i。Let i₆ point downward through 6 Ω. The 6 A entering the upper node splits into i and i₆.

i6=6−i

中节点列KCLApply KCL at the middle node

设18 Ω中的电流向下为i₁₈。i₂=-2 A已经包含参考方向的信息，因此i₁₈=i₆+i₂=4-i。Let i₁₈ point downward through 18 Ω. The value i₂ = -2 A already includes its reference-direction sign, so i₁₈ = i₆ + i₂ = 4 - i.

i18=i6+i2=4−i

同起点、同终点的电压相等Equate voltage drops between the same nodes

上到下经12 Ω的电压降为12i；经6 Ω和18 Ω的总电压降为6(6-i)+18(4-i)。The voltage drop from upper to lower is 12i through 12 Ω and 6(6-i) + 18(4-i) through the other path.

12i=6(6−i)+18(4−i)

解方程并解释方向Solve and interpret the direction

整理得36i=108，故i=3 A。正号表示实际方向与图中向下的箭头一致。Rearranging gives 36i = 108, so i = 3 A. The positive result agrees with the downward arrow.

i=3A

i=3 A，沿12 Ω电阻向下。i = 3 A, downward through the 12 Ω resistor.

易错点Common errors

  • i₂=-2 A表示中间端口实际流出2 A，KCL中保留负号。i₂ = -2 A means 2 A actually leaves the middle port; retain the negative sign in KCL.
  • 三个3 Ω电阻的电流已给定或可由整体KCL确定，求i不需要先算它们的压降。The port currents are given or follow from overall KCL; their 3 Ω voltage drops are unnecessary for finding i.

核验依据Verification

第二种方法：以下节点为0 V独立列节点方程，得上节点36 V、中节点18 V，故i=36/12=3 A。内部电流i₆=3 A、i₁₈=1 A满足全部KCL；书第332页（PDF341页）答案亦为3 A。Independent nodal check with the lower node at 0 V gives upper 36 V and middle 18 V, hence i = 36/12 = 3 A. Internal currents i₆ = 3 A and i₁₈ = 1 A satisfy every KCL equation. The answer on printed p. 332 (PDF p. 341) is also 3 A.
