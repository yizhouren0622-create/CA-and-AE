含电压源的反馈电路：先定输入端电位Feedback with voltage sources: find input-node voltages first

题目原文Transcribed question

试分别求出图题2.3.2中各运放电路的V_O电压值。Find the output voltage V_O of each op-amp circuit in Fig. 2.3.2.

四种负反馈电路。(a)同相端接地，反相端与10 kΩ、20 kΩ分压节点V₁之间串联2 V源（V₁为正端）；(b)同相端接+2 V，反相端接同一分压节点；(c)同相端接地，输出经20 kΩ和2 V源反馈到反相端，源上端为正；(d)同相端接+2 V，输出经20 kΩ反馈到反相端。Four negative-feedback circuits. (a) The non-inverting input is grounded; a 2 V source, positive at divider node V₁, connects V₁ to the inverting input; V₁ is between 10 kΩ to ground and 20 kΩ to the output. (b) The non-inverting input is at +2 V and the inverting input connects directly to the same divider. (c) The grounded non-inverting input is paired with feedback through 20 kΩ and a 2 V source positive at its upper terminal. (d) The non-inverting input is at +2 V and feedback uses 20 kΩ.

先想：怎么识别与起步Hint: recognition and first step

前置知识Prerequisites

理想运放虚短和虚断；电压源极性；KCL。Ideal op-amp virtual short and zero input currents; voltage-source polarity; KCL.
不要先套同相/反相放大器增益公式。先由同相端求反相端电位，再沿反馈路径用电压源极性和零输入电流求输出。First infer the inverting-input potential from the non-inverting input. Then use source polarity and zero input current along the feedback path to find the output.
完整解析与结果核验Full solution & verification

共同起点：虚短、虚断Start with ideal-input conditions

四图都为负反馈。理想线性运放满足V_N=V_P，且流入两个输入端的电流均为零。串在反馈中的独立电压源仍保留其规定电压。All four circuits use negative feedback. In ideal linear operation, V_N = V_P and both input currents are zero. An independent voltage source in the feedback path retains its specified voltage.

VN=VP,iN=iP=0

(a)：电压源使分压节点为2 V(a): the source sets the divider node to 2 V

V_P=0，所以V_N=0。2 V源正端在V₁，故V₁=2 V。在V₁列KCL，流入运放支路电流为零，得到V_O=6 V。V_P = 0 implies V_N = 0. The 2 V source is positive at V₁, so V₁ = 2 V. KCL at V₁, with no current into the input branch, gives V_O = 6 V.

V1=VN+2=2V,210kΩ+2−VO20kΩ=0⇒VO=6V

(b)：直接由虚短确定分压节点(b): the virtual short sets the divider node

同相端为2 V，故V₁=V_N=2 V。与(a)相同的分压网络给出V_O=6 V。The non-inverting input is 2 V, so V₁ = V_N = 2 V. The same divider as in (a) gives V_O = 6 V.

VO=(1+2010)2=6V

(c)：反馈电阻没有压降(c): the feedback resistor has no voltage drop

V_N=V_P=0。反相端支路没有其他通路，电流为零，所以20 kΩ上压降为零。2 V源上端比V_N高2 V，因此输出为2 V。V_N = V_P = 0. The inverting-input branch has no other current path, so its current is zero and the 20 kΩ resistor has no voltage drop. The upper source terminal is 2 V above V_N, hence the output is 2 V.

IR=0,VO=VN+2=2V

(d)：有反馈电阻的电压跟随器(d): a voltage follower with a feedback resistor

V_N=V_P=2 V，反馈电阻电流为零，输出与反相端电位相等，故V_O=2 V。V_N = V_P = 2 V. With zero current in the feedback resistor, the output equals the inverting-input potential, giving V_O = 2 V.

VO=VN=VP=2V

(a)6 V；(b)6 V；(c)2 V；(d)2 V。(a) 6 V; (b) 6 V; (c) 2 V; (d) 2 V.

易错点Common errors

  • 虚短是两个输入端电位相等，不是把串联的2 V源短路。A virtual short equates the input potentials; it does not short the series 2 V source.
  • (c)(d)反馈电阻电流为零；不能把它们错当作带接地输入电阻的普通放大器。The feedback-resistor current in (c) and (d) is zero; these circuits do not have the grounded input resistor of a conventional gain network.

Independent substitution into KCL gives 0.2 mA in both divider resistors for (a) and (b), and zero feedback current for (c) and (d). All results satisfy V_N = V_P.
