1. 参考方向与功率1. References and power

10分10 points

元件电压参考极性为左正右负，电流参考方向为从左到右。给定u = −12 V，i = 2 A。求吸收功率并说明供出还是吸收。若仅把电流参考方向反向，新的电流值和功率表达式是什么？An element has voltage referenced positive on the left and current referenced left to right. Given u = −12 V and i = 2 A, find absorbed power. Reverse only the current reference: what are the new current value and power expression?

提示Hint
先判定是否为关联参考方向。Check the passive sign convention first.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 原参考为关联方向，pabs=ui=(−12)(2)=−24W，即供出24 W。Originally passive: pabs=ui=(−12)(2)=−24W, so 24 W is delivered.
  2. 只翻转电流箭头后，i′=−2A，变为非关联方向，pabs=−ui′=−24W。Reversing only the current arrow gives i′=−2A and the non-passive relation pabs=−ui′=−24W.

吸收功率−24 W；新电流−2 A。Absorbed power −24 W; new current −2 A.

改变参考方向不改变实际功率。评分：方向判断3分，原功率3分，新参考表达式4分。Changing a reference cannot change physical power. Rubric: convention 3, original power 3, reversed-reference relation 4.

2. 单节点列方程2. One-node analysis

10分10 points

节点a通过2 kΩ接到+12 V节点，通过4 kΩ接地；另有从地指向a的1 mA理想电流源。求Va及两电阻从a流出的电流。Node a connects to +12 V through 2 kilohms and to ground through 4 kilohms. A 1 mA ideal current source points from ground to a. Find Va and the resistor currents leaving a.

提示Hint
流出电阻电流之和等于注入的1 mA。Sum resistor currents leaving a and equate to the 1 mA injection.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 写KCL：(Va−12)/2000+Va/4000=0.001。KCL: (Va−12)/2000+Va/4000=0.001.
  2. 乘4000得2(Va−12)+Va=4，故Va=28/3V。Multiply by 4000: 2(Va−12)+Va=4, so Va=28/3V.
  3. i2k=−4/3mA，i4k=7/3mA。i2k=−4/3mA and i4k=7/3mA.

Va = 9.333… V；流出电流−1.333… mA、2.333… mA。Va = 9.333… V; leaving currents −1.333… mA and 2.333… mA.

电流和为1 mA；a点电位低于12 V，高于地。评分：KCL4分，电压3分，电流与符号3分。Currents sum to 1 mA, and the node lies between ground and 12 V. Rubric: KCL 4, voltage 3, signed currents 3.

3. 浮置电压源3. Floating voltage source

10分10 points

节点a经2 kΩ接地，节点b经3 kΩ接地；两节点之间有理想电压源，约束Va−Vb = 6 V。另有从地向b注入1 mA的电流源。求Va、Vb。Node a connects to ground through 2 kilohms, b through 3 kilohms. A floating ideal source imposes Va−Vb = 6 V. A source injects 1 mA from ground into b. Find Va and Vb.

提示Hint
把a、b合成超节点，电压源支路电流在整体KCL中抵消。Use a supernode; the internal voltage-source current cancels from its KCL.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 超节点KCL：Va/2000+Vb/3000=0.001；约束Va−Vb=6。Supernode KCL: Va/2000+Vb/3000=0.001, with Va−Vb=6.
  2. 代入Va=Vb+6，得3(Vb+6)+2Vb=6。Substitute Va=Vb+6: 3(Vb+6)+2Vb=6.
  3. Vb=−2.4V，Va=3.6V。Vb=−2.4V and Va=3.6V.

Va = 3.6 V，Vb = −2.4 V。Va = 3.6 V and Vb = −2.4 V.

1.8 mA−0.8 mA = 1 mA；电压差6 V。评分：超节点方程4分，约束2分，求解4分。1.8 mA−0.8 mA = 1 mA, and the voltage difference is 6 V. Rubric: supernode 4, constraint 2, solution 4.

4. 叠加不能直接叠功率4. Superposition and power

10分10 points

节点a经2 kΩ接+10 V，经4 kΩ接地；2 mA电流源从地注入a。分别求仅电压源、仅电流源作用时Va，再求全电路4 kΩ电阻的功率。两个单独功率能否直接相加？Node a connects to +10 V through 2 kilohms and to ground through 4 kilohms; a 2 mA source injects from ground. Find Va due to each source alone and the total power in 4 kilohms. Can the separate powers be added?

提示Hint
独立电流源置零开路，独立电压源置零短路。Zero the current source by opening it and the voltage source by shorting it.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 仅10 V源：电流源开路，Va1=10(4/6)=20/3V。Voltage source alone: open the current source; Va1=10(4/6)=20/3V.
  2. 仅2 mA源：10 V源短路，Va2=2mA(2k∥4k)=8/3V。Current source alone: short the voltage source; Va2=2mA(2k∥4k)=8/3V.
  3. Va=28/3V，P=Va2/(4k)=196/9mW。Va=28/3V and P=Va2/(4k)=196/9mW.
  4. 单独功率之和100/9+16/9=116/9mW不等于总功率，缺少交叉项。Separate powers sum to 116/9mW, not the total; the cross term is missing.

Va1 = 6.667 V，Va2 = 2.667 V；总功率约21.778 mW；功率不能直接叠加。Va1 = 6.667 V, Va2 = 2.667 V; total power about 21.778 mW. Power does not superpose.

用原电路KCL独立复算Va。评分：各分量各3分，总功率2分，不能叠功率的理由2分。Independently check Va with original KCL. Rubric: source components 3 each, total power 2, explanation 2.

叠加、戴维南与最大功率Superposition, Thevenin and maximum power

5. 端口等效与最大功率5. Port equivalent and maximum power

10分10 points

理想12 V源给2 kΩ与4 kΩ串联分压，输出端口为4 kΩ两端。另接可变负载RL到输出端口。求戴维南等效、匹配负载与最大负载功率。An ideal 12 V source feeds series 2 and 4 kilohm resistors. The output port is across the 4 kilohm resistor. An additional variable RL connects across this port. Find the Thevenin equivalent, matched load and maximum load power.

提示Hint
卸下的是外接RL，原4 kΩ仍属于源网络。Remove the external RL; keep the original 4 kilohm resistor in the source network.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 开路端电压UTh=12(4/6)=8V。Open-port voltage UTh=12(4/6)=8V.
  2. 12 V源置零短路，RTh=2k∥4k=4/3kΩ。Short the 12 V source: RTh=2k∥4k=4/3kΩ.
  3. RL=RTh时负载电压为4 V，Pmax=82/(4RTh)=12mW。At RL=RTh, load voltage is 4 V and Pmax=82/(4RTh)=12mW.

UTh = 8 V，RTh = RL = 4/3 kΩ；Pmax = 12 mW。UTh = 8 V, RTh = RL = 4/3 kilohms; Pmax = 12 mW.

用负载并入原4 kΩ后的分压检验输出4 V。评分：开路3分，电阻3分，匹配与功率4分。Check 4 V using the loaded original divider. Rubric: open-circuit voltage 3, resistance 3, matching/power 4.

6. RC三要素6. RC three-parameter response

10分10 points

10 μF电容初始电压为2 V。t = 0后，它经2 kΩ电阻接到理想10 V直流源，电容下端接地，上端为正。求uC(t)、充电电流i(t)、时间常数以及t = τ时电容电压。A 10 microfarad capacitor starts at 2 V, positive at its upper terminal. At t = 0 it connects to an ideal 10 V DC source through 2 kilohms, with its lower terminal grounded. Find uC(t), charging current, tau and uC(tau).

提示Hint
uC(0+) = 2 V，uC(∞) = 10 V。uC(0+) = 2 V and uC(infinity) = 10 V.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. τ=RC=0.020s。τ=RC=0.020s.
  2. uC(t)=10−8e−t/0.020V, t≥0。uC(t)=10−8e−t/0.020V, t≥0.
  3. 从电源流入电容正端的电流i(t)=(10−uC)/2000=4e−t/0.020mA。Current entering the positive capacitor terminal is i(t)=(10−uC)/2000=4e−t/0.020mA.
  4. uC(τ)=10−8/e≈7.05696V。uC(τ)=10−8/e≈7.05696V.

τ = 20 ms；uC = 10−8e^(−t/20ms) V；i = 4e^(−t/20ms) mA。Tau = 20 ms; uC = 10−8e^(−t/20ms) V; i = 4e^(−t/20ms) mA.

代入t = 0、∞；另用i = C du/dt得到相同电流。评分：τ2分，电压3分，电流3分，τ处值2分。Check t = 0 and infinity; i = C du/dt independently gives the same current. Rubric: tau 2, voltage 3, current 3, one-tau value 2.

7. RL初值与电感电压7. RL current and inductor voltage

10分10 points

0.2 H理想电感初始电流为0。t = 0后与100 Ω电阻串联接10 V直流源。电流从源正端经电阻进入电感正参考端。求iL(t)、uL(t)和时间常数。A 0.2 H ideal inductor has zero initial current. At t = 0 it connects in series with 100 ohms across 10 V DC. Current enters the positive inductor-voltage reference terminal. Find iL(t), uL(t) and tau.

提示Hint
终值电流10/100 A；τ = L/R。Final current is 10/100 A; tau = L/R.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. τ=0.2/100=0.002s。τ=0.2/100=0.002s.
  2. iL(t)=0.1(1−e−t/0.002)A。iL(t)=0.1(1−e−t/0.002)A.
  3. uL(t)=LdiL/dt=10e−t/0.002V。uL(t)=LdiL/dt=10e−t/0.002V.

τ = 2 ms；iL = 0.1(1−e^(−t/2ms)) A；uL = 10e^(−t/2ms) V。Tau = 2 ms; iL = 0.1(1−e^(−t/2ms)) A; uL = 10e^(−t/2ms) V.

对所有t都有100iL + uL = 10 V。评分：τ2分，电流4分，电压及方向4分。For all t, 100iL + uL = 10 V. Rubric: tau 2, current 4, voltage/reference 4.

8. 有效值相量与回到时域8. RMS phasors back to time domain

10分10 points

电压u(t)=102cos(1000t+30∘)V施加于串联阻抗Z=6+j8Ω。求有效值电流相量和时域电流，电流进入阻抗的电压正参考端。Apply u(t)=102cos(1000t+30∘)V to Z=6+j8Ω. Find the RMS current phasor and time-domain current using passive references.

提示Hint
先把阻抗转成模与角度；电流相位是电压相位减阻抗角。Convert impedance to magnitude/angle; subtract impedance phase from voltage phase.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. U―=10∠30∘V，Z=10∠53.130102∘Ω。U―=10∠30∘V and Z=10∠53.130102∘Ω.
  2. I―=1∠(−23.130102∘)A。I―=1∠(−23.130102∘)A.
  3. i(t)=2cos(1000t−23.130102∘)A。i(t)=2cos(1000t−23.130102∘)A.

I = 1∠−23.1301° A（有效值）；时域峰值√2 A。I = 1 angle −23.1301 degrees A RMS; time-domain peak is sqrt(2) A.

阻抗感性，电流滞后电压53.1301°；复乘ZI回到U。评分：电压约定2分，阻抗2分，相量3分，时域3分。Inductive impedance gives current lag of 53.1301 degrees; ZI recovers U. Rubric: convention 2, impedance 2, phasor 3, time-domain 3.

9. 电容阻抗与串联等效9. Capacitive impedance and series equivalent

10分10 points

在ω=1000rad/s时，串联电路含R = 3 Ω、L = 4 mH、C = 1 mF，外加有效值相量U―=6∠0∘V。求总阻抗与电流相量。At ω=1000rad/s, a series circuit contains R = 3 ohms, L = 4 mH and C = 1 mF, driven by RMS U―=6∠0∘V. Find total impedance and current.

提示Hint
电容阻抗是−j/(ωC)，不是+j/(ωC)。Capacitor impedance is −j/(omega C), not positive j/(omega C).
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. ZL=j4Ω,ZC=−j1Ω。ZL=j4Ω,ZC=−j1Ω.
  2. Z=3+j3=32∠45∘Ω。Z=3+j3=32∠45∘Ω.
  3. I―=(1−j)A=2∠(−45∘)A。I―=(1−j)A=2∠(−45∘)A.

Z = 3+j3 Ω；I = 1−j A（有效值）。Z = 3+j3 ohms; I = 1−j A RMS.

(3+j3)(1−j) = 6；单位rad/s不需再乘2π。评分：元件阻抗4分，总阻抗3分，电流3分。(3+j3)(1−j) = 6. The given rad/s needs no extra 2 pi. Rubric: element impedances 4, total 3, current 3.

10. RC频率特性概念10. RC frequency-response concepts

10分10 points

电压源经串联R到节点a，a经电容C接地，输出取a对地电压。说明低频与高频极限、滤波类型、截止处相对通带的幅度，以及R增大时截止频率如何变化。本题只考概念。A voltage source connects through R to node a, with C from a to ground; output is a-to-ground voltage. Explain low/high-frequency limits, filter type, cutoff magnitude relative to the passband, and the effect of increasing R. Concepts only.

提示Hint
在ω → 0时电容近似开路，在ω → ∞时近似短路。C approaches an open at low frequency and a short at high frequency.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 低频时电容近似开路，电阻电流趋零，输出趋近输入。At low frequency, capacitor current approaches zero and output approaches input.
  2. 高频时电容近似短路，输出趋近0，所以是低通。At high frequency, C shorts the output toward ground: low-pass behavior.
  3. 截止处相对幅度1/2≈0.7071，约−3 dB。At cutoff, relative magnitude is 1/2≈0.7071, about −3 dB.
  4. fc=1/(2πRC)，R增大则截止频率降低。fc=1/(2πRC) decreases as R increases.

低通；低频约1、高频约0；截止幅度0.707；R增大则fc降低。Low-pass; low-frequency ratio 1, high-frequency ratio 0; cutoff magnitude 0.707; larger R lowers fc.
