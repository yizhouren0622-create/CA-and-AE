1. 参考方向与功率1. References and power

10分10 points

元件电压参考极性为左正右负，电流参考方向为从左到右。给定u=−12V，i=2A。求吸收功率并说明供出还是吸收。若仅把电流参考方向反向，新的电流值和功率表达式是什么？An element has voltage referenced positive on the left and current referenced left to right. Given u=−12V and i=2A, find absorbed power. Reverse only the current reference: what are the new current value and power expression?

提示Hint
先判定是否为关联参考方向。Check the passive sign convention first.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 原参考为关联方向，pabs=ui=(−12)(2)=−24W，即供出24W。Originally passive: pabs=ui=(−12)(2)=−24W, so 24W is delivered.
  2. 只翻转电流箭头后，i′=−2A，变为非关联方向，pabs=−ui′=−24W。Reversing only the current arrow gives i′=−2A and the non-passive relation pabs=−ui′=−24W.

吸收功率−24W；新电流−2A。Absorbed power −24W; new current −2A.

改变参考方向不改变实际功率。评分：方向判断3分，原功率3分，新参考表达式4分。Changing a reference cannot change physical power. Rubric: convention 3, original power 3, reversed-reference relation 4.

2. 单节点列方程2. One-node analysis

10分10 points

节点a通过2kΩ接到+12V节点，通过4kΩ接地；另有从地指向a的1mA理想电流源。求Va及两电阻从a流出的电流。Node a connects to +12V through 2kilohms and to ground through 4kilohms. A1mA ideal current source points from ground to a. Find Va and the resistor currents leaving a.

提示Hint
流出电阻电流之和等于注入的1mA。Sum resistor currents leaving a and equate to the 1mA injection.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 写KCL：(Va−12)2000+Va4000=0.001。KCL: (Va−12)2000+Va4000=0.001.
  2. 乘4000得2(Va−12)+Va=4，故Va=283V。Multiply by 4000: 2(Va−12)+Va=4, so Va=283V.
  3. i2k=−43mA，i4k=73mA。i2k=−43mA and i4k=73mA.

Va=9.333… V；流出电流−1.333… mA、2.333… mA。Va=9.333… V; leaving currents −1.333… mA and 2.333… mA.

电流和为1mA；a点电位低于12V，高于地。评分：KCL4分，电压3分，电流与符号3分。Currents sum to 1mA, and the node lies between ground and 12V. Rubric: KCL 4, voltage 3, signed currents 3.

3. 浮置电压源3. Floating voltage source

10分10 points

节点a经2kΩ接地，节点b经3kΩ接地；两节点之间有理想电压源，约束Va−Vb=6V。另有从地向b注入1mA的电流源。求Va、Vb。Node a connects to ground through 2kilohms, b through 3kilohms. A floating ideal source imposes Va−Vb=6V. A source injects 1mA from ground into b. Find Va and Vb.

提示Hint
把a、b合成超节点，电压源支路电流在整体KCL中抵消。Use a supernode; the internal voltage-source current cancels from its KCL.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 超节点KCL：Va2000+Vb3000=0.001；约束Va−Vb=6。Supernode KCL: Va2000+Vb3000=0.001, with Va−Vb=6.
  2. 代入Va=Vb+6，得3(Vb+6)+2Vb=6。Substitute Va=Vb+6: 3(Vb+6)+2Vb=6.
  3. Vb=−2.4V，Va=3.6V。Vb=−2.4V and Va=3.6V.

Va=3.6V，Vb=−2.4V。Va=3.6V and Vb=−2.4V.

1.8mA−0.8mA=1mA；电压差6V。评分：超节点方程4分，约束2分，求解4分。1.8mA−0.8mA=1mA, and the voltage difference is 6V. Rubric: supernode 4, constraint 2, solution 4.

4. 叠加不能直接叠功率4. Superposition and power

10分10 points

节点a经2kΩ接+10V，经4kΩ接地；2mA电流源从地注入a。分别求仅电压源、仅电流源作用时Va，再求全电路4kΩ电阻的功率。两个单独功率能否直接相加？Node a connects to +10V through 2kilohms and to ground through 4kilohms; a2mA source injects from ground. Find Va due to each source alone and the total power in 4kilohms. Can the separate powers be added?

提示Hint
独立电流源置零开路，独立电压源置零短路。Zero the current source by opening it and the voltage source by shorting it.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 仅10V源：电流源开路，Va1=10(46)=203V。Voltage source alone: open the current source; Va1=10(46)=203V.
  2. 仅2mA源：10V源短路，Va2=2mA(2k∥4k)=83V。Current source alone: short the voltage source; Va2=2mA(2k∥4k)=83V.
  3. Va=283V，P=Va2(4k)=1969mW。Va=283V and P=Va2(4k)=1969mW.
  4. 单独功率之和1009+169=1169mW不等于总功率，缺少交叉项。Separate powers sum to 1169mW, not the total; the cross term is missing.

Va1=6.667V，Va2=2.667V；总功率约21.778mW；功率不能直接叠加。Va1=6.667V, Va2=2.667V; total power about 21.778mW. Power does not superpose.

用原电路KCL独立复算Va。评分：各分量各3分，总功率2分，不能叠功率的理由2分。Independently check Va with original KCL. Rubric: source components 3 each, total power 2, explanation 2.

5. 端口等效与最大功率5. Port equivalent and maximum power

10分10 points

理想12V源给2kΩ与4kΩ串联分压，输出端口为4kΩ两端。另接可变负载RL到输出端口。求戴维南等效、匹配负载与最大负载功率。An ideal 12V source feeds series 2 and 4kilohm resistors. The output port is across the 4kilohm resistor. An additional variable RL connects across this port. Find the Thevenin equivalent, matched load and maximum load power.

提示Hint
卸下的是外接RL，原4kΩ仍属于源网络。Remove the external RL; keep the original 4kilohm resistor in the source network.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 开路端电压UTh=12(46)=8V。Open-port voltage UTh=12(46)=8V.
  2. 12V源置零短路，RTh=2k∥4k=43kΩ。Short the 12V source: RTh=2k∥4k=43kΩ.
  3. RL=RTh时负载电压为4V，Pmax=82(4RTh)=12mW。At RL=RTh, load voltage is 4V and Pmax=82(4RTh)=12mW.

Uth=8V，Rth=RL=43kΩ；Pmax=12mW。Uth=8V, Rth=RL=43kilohms; Pmax=12mW.

用负载并入原4kΩ后的分压检验输出4V。评分：开路3分，电阻3分，匹配与功率4分。Check 4V using the loaded original divider. Rubric: open-circuit voltage 3, resistance 3, matching and power 4.

6.RC三要素6.RC three-parameter response

10分10 points

10μF电容初始电压为2V。t=0后，它经2kΩ电阻接到理想10V直流源，电容下端接地，上端为正。求uC(t)、充电电流i(t)、时间常数以及t=τ时电容电压。A10 microfarad capacitor starts at 2V, positive at its upper terminal. At t=0 it connects to an ideal 10V DC source through 2kilohms, with its lower terminal grounded. Find uC(t), charging current, τ and uC(τ).

提示Hint
uC(0+)=2V，uC(∞)=10V。uC(0+)=2V and uC(infinity) =10V.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. τ=RC=0.020s。τ=RC=0.020s.
  2. uC(t)=10−8e−t0.020V, t≥0。uC(t)=10−8e−t0.020V, t≥0.
  3. 从电源流入电容正端的电流i(t)=(10−uC)2000=4e−t0.020mA。Current entering the positive capacitor terminal is i(t)=(10−uC)2000=4e−t0.020mA.
  4. uC(τ)=10−8e≈7.05696V。uC(τ)=10−8e≈7.05696V.

τ=20ms；uC=10−8e−t20msV；i=4e−t20msmA。τ=20ms; uC=10−8e−t20msV; i=4e−t20msmA.

代入t=0、∞；另用i=Cdudt得到相同电流。评分：τ2分，电压3分，电流3分，τ处值2分。Check t=0 and infinity; i=Cdudt independently gives the same current. Rubric: τ2, voltage 3, current 3, one−τ value 2.

7.RL初值与电感电压7.RL current and inductor voltage

10分10 points

0.2H理想电感初始电流为0。t=0后与100Ω电阻串联接10V直流源。电流从源正端经电阻进入电感正参考端。求iL(t)、uL(t)和时间常数。A0.2H ideal inductor has zero initial current. At t=0 it connects in series with 100ohms across 10V DC. Current enters the positive inductor-voltage reference terminal. Find iL(t), uL(t) and τ.

提示Hint
终值电流10100A；τ=LR。Final current is 10100A; τ=LR.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. τ=0.2100=0.002s。τ=0.2100=0.002s.
  2. iL(t)=0.1(1−e−t0.002)A。iL(t)=0.1(1−e−t0.002)A.
  3. uL(t)=LdiLdt=10e−t0.002V。uL(t)=LdiLdt=10e−t0.002V.

8. 有效值相量与回到时域8. RMS phasors back to time domain

10分10 points

电压u(t)=102cos(1000t+30∘)V施加于串联阻抗Z=6+j8Ω。求有效值电流相量和时域电流，电流进入阻抗的电压正参考端。Apply u(t)=102cos(1000t+30∘)V to Z=6+j8Ω. Find the RMS current phasor and time-domain current using passive references.

提示Hint
先把阻抗转成模与角度；电流相位是电压相位减阻抗角。Convert impedance to magnitude and angle; subtract impedance phase from voltage phase.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. U―=10∠30∘V，Z=10∠53.130102∘Ω。U―=10∠30∘V and Z=10∠53.130102∘Ω.
  2. I―=1∠(−23.130102∘)A。I―=1∠(−23.130102∘)A.
  3. i(t)=2cos(1000t−23.130102∘)A。i(t)=2cos(1000t−23.130102∘)A.

I=1∠−23.1301°A（有效值）；时域峰值2A。I=1 angle −23.1301 degrees A RMS; time-domain peak is 2A.

阻抗感性，电流滞后电压53.1301°；复乘ZI回到U。评分：电压约定2分，阻抗2分，相量3分，时域3分。Inductive impedance gives current lag of 53.1301 degrees; ZI recovers U. Rubric: convention 2, impedance 2, phasor 3, time-domain 3.

9. 电容阻抗与串联等效9. Capacitive impedance and series equivalent

10分10 points

在ω=1000rads时，串联电路含R=3Ω、L=4mH、C=1mF，外加有效值相量U―=6∠0∘V。求总阻抗与电流相量。At ω=1000rads, a series circuit contains R=3ohms, L=4mH and C=1mF, driven by RMS U―=6∠0∘V. Find total impedance and current.

提示Hint
电容阻抗是−j(ωC)，不是+j(ωC)。Capacitor impedance is −j(ωC), not positive j(ωC).
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. ZL=j4Ω,ZC=−j1Ω。ZL=j4Ω,ZC=−j1Ω.
  2. Z=3+j3=32∠45∘Ω。Z=3+j3=32∠45∘Ω.
  3. I―=(1−j)A=2∠(−45∘)A。I―=(1−j)A=2∠(−45∘)A.

Z=3+j3Ω；I=1−jA（有效值）。Z=3+j3ohms; I=1−jA RMS.

(3+j3)(1−j)=6；单位rads不需再乘2π。评分：元件阻抗4分，总阻抗3分，电流3分。(3+j3)(1−j)=6. The given rads needs no extra 2π. Rubric: element impedances 4, total 3, current 3.

10.RC频率特性概念10.RC frequency-response concepts

10分10 points

电压源经串联R到节点a，a经电容C接地，输出取a对地电压。说明低频与高频极限、滤波类型、截止处相对通带的幅度，以及R增大时截止频率如何变化。本题只考概念。A voltage source connects through R to node a, with C from a to ground; output is a−to-ground voltage. Explain low and high-frequency limits, filter type, cutoff magnitude relative to the passband, and the effect of increasing R. Concepts only.

提示Hint
在ω→0时电容近似开路，在ω→∞时近似短路。C approaches an open at low frequency and a short at high frequency.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 低频时电容近似开路，电阻电流趋零，输出趋近输入。At low frequency, capacitor current approaches zero and output approaches input.
  2. 高频时电容近似短路，输出趋近0，所以是低通。At high frequency, C shorts the output toward ground: low-pass behavior.
  3. 截止处相对幅度12≈0.7071，约−3dB。At cutoff, relative magnitude is 12≈0.7071, about −3dB.
  4. fc=1(2πRC)，R增大则截止频率降低。fc=1(2πRC) decreases as R increases.

低通；低频约1、高频约0；截止幅度0.707；R增大则fc降低。Low-pass; low-frequency ratio 1, high-frequency ratio 0; cutoff magnitude 0.707; larger R lowers fc.
