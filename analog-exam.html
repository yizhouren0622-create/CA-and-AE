1. 加载后的源电压增益1. Loaded source-to-output gain

10分10 points

信号源vs=10mV，内阻Rs=2kΩ。放大器输入电阻Ri=8kΩ，空载电压增益Av0=−20，输出电阻Ro=1kΩ，负载RL=4kΩ。求vi、vo和源电压增益。A10mV source has Rs=2kilohms. The amplifier has Ri=8kilohms, unloaded voltage gain −20 and Ro=1kilohm. With RL=4kilohms, find vi, vo and source-to-output gain.

提示Hint
输入和输出各有一次分压。Include an input divider and an output divider.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. vi=10mV·8(2+8)=8mV。vi=10mV·8(2+8)=8mV.
  2. 空载输出=−20(8)=−160mV；带载vo=−160·4(1+4)=−128mV。Unloaded output is −160mV; loaded vo=−160·4(1+4)=−128mV.
  3. Avs=vovs=−12.8。Avs=vovs=−12.8.

vi=8mV，vo=−128mV，Avs=−12.8。vi=8mV, vo=−128mV, Avs=−12.8.

两次分压均为0.8，0.8×(−20)×0.8 = −12.8。评分：输入3分，输出4分，总增益3分。Both dividers are 0.8, so 0.8×(−20)×0.8 = −12.8. Rubric: input 3, output 4, overall gain 3.

  3. |vo|≤12给|vi|≤1.2V；实际保持严格线性应避开边界。|vo|≤12 implies |vi|≤1.2V; stay inside the boundary for strict linear operation.

vo=−2V，Rin支路输入电流20μA；理想边界|vi|=1.2V。 理想运放输入管脚电流为0。vo=−2V, Rin branch current 20 microamps; ideal input boundary |vi|=1.2V. The ideal op-amp input-pin current is zero.

提示Hint

  3. 反向电压未达到给定击穿条件，截止成立。The reverse voltage is below the stated breakdown condition, so cutoff is consistent.

正接4.3mA，反接0；反接时vD=−5V。Forward 4.3mA; reversed zero, with vD=−5V.

  3. VI=VZ+R(IL+IZ)，故7V≤VI≤16V。VI=VZ+R(IL+IZ) gives 7V≤VI≤16V.

IR=7mA，IL=1mA，IZ=6mA，PZ=30mW；Vi范围7–16V。IR=7mA, IL=1mA, IZ=6mA, PZ=30mW; Vi range 7–16V.

8. 有限增益与深负反馈近似8. Finite gain and deep-feedback approximation

  3. 相对误差[(1009)−(100091)](100091)=190≈1.1111%。The relative error is 190≈1.1111%.

10. 迟滞比较器与概念边界10. Hysteresis comparator and concept checks

10分10 points

理想反相迟滞比较器：信号vi接反相端，同相端v+=0.2vo，输出电平±10V。求上门限、下门限、回差；说明从vi=−3V逐渐上升到3V时如何翻转。最后判断：两个差分输入都增加同样的Δ属于共模还是差模？An ideal inverting Schmitt trigger has vi at the inverting input, v+=0.2vo and output levels ±10V. 提示Hint

答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks

  3. 回差2−(−2)=4V。从−3V上升时先高，越过+2V后低。Hysteresis is 4V. Increasing from −3V starts high and switches low past +2V.
  4. 两个输入同增Δ使差值不变，属于共模变化；仅需概念判断。Equal input increments leave their difference unchanged: common-mode change, assessed conceptually.
