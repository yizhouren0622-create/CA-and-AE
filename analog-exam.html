1. 加载后的源电压增益1. Loaded source-to-output gain

10分10 points

信号源vs = 10 mV，内阻Rs = 2 kΩ。放大器输入电阻Ri = 8 kΩ，空载电压增益Av0 = −20，输出电阻Ro = 1 kΩ，负载RL = 4 kΩ。求vi、vo和源电压增益。A 10 mV source has Rs = 2 kilohms. The amplifier has Ri = 8 kilohms, unloaded voltage gain −20 and Ro = 1 kilohm. With RL = 4 kilohms, find vi, vo and source-to-output gain.

提示Hint
输入和输出各有一次分压。Include an input divider and an output divider.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. vi=10mV·8/(2+8)=8mV。vi=10mV·8/(2+8)=8mV.
  2. 空载输出=−20(8)=−160mV；带载vo=−160·4/(1+4)=−128mV。Unloaded output is −160mV; loaded vo=−160·4/(1+4)=−128mV.
  3. Avs=vo/vs=−12.8。Avs=vo/vs=−12.8.

vi = 8 mV，vo = −128 mV，Avs = −12.8。vi = 8 mV, vo = −128 mV, Avs = −12.8.

两次分压均为0.8，0.8×(−20)×0.8 = −12.8。评分：输入3分，输出4分，总增益3分。Both dividers are 0.8, so 0.8×(−20)×0.8 = −12.8. Rubric: input 3, output 4, overall gain 3.

2. 反相放大与饱和检查2. Inverting amplifier and saturation check

10分10 points

理想运放同相端接地，输入经10 kΩ接反相端，100 kΩ反馈电阻接输出到反相端。假设可用输出范围±12 V，输入vi = 0.2 V。求vo、Rin支路输入电流及保持线性时|vi|的上限。An ideal op-amp has its non-inverting input grounded, Rin = 10 kilohms and Rf = 100 kilohms. Assume output limits ±12 V. For vi = 0.2 V, find vo, Rin branch current and maximum |vi| for linear operation.

提示Hint
负反馈线性时反相节点为虚地；输出需在±12 V内。Under linear negative feedback the inverting node is virtual ground; check ±12 V output limits.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 线性候选解v−=v+=0，输入电流i=(0.2−0)/10k=20μA。Candidate linear solution: v−=v+=0, so input current is 20μA.
  2. KCL：(0−vi)/10k+(0−vo)/100k=0，得vo=−10vi=−2V。KCL gives (0−vi)/10k+(0−vo)/100k=0, hence vo=−10vi=−2V.
  3. |vo|≤12给|vi|≤1.2V；实际保持严格线性应避开边界。|vo|≤12 implies |vi|≤1.2V; stay inside the boundary for strict linear operation.

vo = −2 V，Rin支路输入电流20 μA；理想边界|vi| = 1.2 V。 理想运放输入管脚电流为0。vo = −2 V, Rin branch current 20 microamps; ideal input boundary |vi| = 1.2 V. The ideal op-amp input-pin current is zero.

反馈电阻电流同为20 μA，候选输出未饱和。评分：虚短条件2分，KCL与vo4分，电流2分，范围2分。Feedback current is also 20 microamps and the candidate output is unsaturated. Rubric: regime 2, KCL/output 4, current 2, limits 2.

3. 积分器及初值3. Integrator and initial condition

10分10 points

理想反相积分器输入电阻10 kΩ、反馈电容0.1 μF，同相端接地，输出初值0 V。t = 0后输入1 V直流。假设输出在±10 V内线性。求2 ms时输出和到达−10 V的理想时间。An ideal inverting integrator has R = 10 kilohms, feedback C = 0.1 microfarad, grounded non-inverting input and initial output 0 V. A 1 V DC input begins at t = 0. Assume linear swing ±10 V. Find output at 2 ms and the ideal time to −10 V.

提示Hint
RC = 1 ms；恒定输入产生负斜率。RC = 1 ms; constant positive input produces a negative output slope.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. RC=104·10−7=10−3s。RC=10−3s.
  2. vo(t)=0−t/(10−3)V=−1000tV（t以秒计，尚未饱和）。vo(t)=−1000tV with t in seconds, until saturation.
  3. vo(2ms)=−2V；−1000t=−10给t=10ms。vo(2ms)=−2V and the ideal boundary time is 10ms.

2 ms时−2 V；10 ms到达负输出边界，之后不延伸线性斜坡。−2 V at 2 ms; reaches the negative boundary at 10 ms, after which the linear ramp cannot continue.

电阻输入电流0.1 mA，经反馈电容产生−1000 V/s斜率。评分：RC2分，含初值关系4分，两结果各2分。The 0.1 mA input current gives −1000 V/s through the feedback capacitor. Rubric: RC 2, initial-value relation 4, each result 2.

4. 二极管状态验算4. Diode state consistency

10分10 points

5 V理想源经1 kΩ串联二极管接地，先使阳极接电阻、阴极接地。采用0.7 V恒压降模型，求电流。再将二极管反接，假定反向击穿电压大于5 V，求电流并验证截止条件。A 5 V source feeds a diode through 1 kilohm to ground, initially with anode at the resistor and cathode grounded. Use a 0.7 V constant-drop model. Find current. Reverse the diode, assume breakdown above 5 V, then find current and check cutoff.

提示Hint
导通解检查电流为正；截止解检查阳极对阴极电压。Check positive current for an on-state and anode-to-cathode voltage for cutoff.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 正接假定导通：ID=(5−0.7)/1k=4.3mA>0，状态成立。Forward candidate: ID=(5−0.7)/1k=4.3mA>0, consistent.
  2. 反接假定截止：ID=0，电阻无压降，阴极5 V、阳极0 V，vD=−5V。Reversed candidate: ID=0; cathode is at 5 V and anode at 0 V, so vD=−5V.
  3. 反向电压未达到给定击穿条件，截止成立。The reverse voltage is below the stated breakdown condition, so cutoff is consistent.

正接4.3 mA，反接0；反接时vD = −5 V。Forward 4.3 mA; reversed zero, with vD = −5 V.

正接KVL 4.3+0.7 = 5 V。评分：模型2分，正接3分，反接3分，状态检查2分。Forward KVL: 4.3+0.7 = 5 V. Rubric: model 2, forward 3, reverse 3, validation 2.

5. 稳压管电流预算5. Zener current budget

10分10 points

稳压电路Vi = 12 V，经R = 1 kΩ接输出；输出并联RL = 5 kΩ与反向稳压管。采用VZ = 5 V恒压模型，正常稳压要求1 mA ≤ IZ ≤ 10 mA。求IR、IL、IZ、PZ，并求保持稳压的输入电压范围。A Zener regulator has Vi = 12 V, series R = 1 kilohm, RL = 5 kilohms and a reverse Zener across the output. Use VZ = 5 V with regulation requiring 1 mA ≤ IZ ≤ 10 mA. Find currents, Zener power and allowable Vi range.

提示Hint
IR = IL + IZ；输入范围用IZ两端限制。IR = IL + IZ; use the two Zener-current limits for Vi.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. IR=(12−5)/1k=7mA，IL=5/5k=1mA。IR=7mA and IL=1mA.
  2. IZ=6mA，PZ=5(6mA)=30mW，电流在范围内。IZ=6mA, giving PZ=30mW; current is within limits.
  3. VI=VZ+R(IL+IZ)，故7V≤VI≤16V。VI=VZ+R(IL+IZ) gives 7V≤VI≤16V.

IR = 7 mA，IL = 1 mA，IZ = 6 mA，PZ = 30 mW；Vi范围7–16 V。IR = 7 mA, IL = 1 mA, IZ = 6 mA, PZ = 30 mW; Vi range 7–16 V.

上下边界分别代回得到1与10 mA。评分：三电流4分，功率2分，范围与验证4分。Substitute both voltage boundaries to recover 1 and 10 mA. Rubric: currents 4, power 2, range/validation 4.

6. NMOS静态与交流6. NMOS DC and small-signal analysis

10分10 points

增强型NMOS源极接地，栅极直流3 V，漏极经2 kΩ接12 V。VT=1V，规定ID=K(VGS−VT)2且K = 1 mA/V²，忽略沟道调制。交流时栅极直接加微小信号vi，漏极通过足够大的耦合电容接2 kΩ负载。求Q点、验证工作区，再求gm和中频电压增益。An enhancement NMOS has grounded source, DC gate 3 V and drain through 2 kilohms to 12 V. Let VT=1V and ID=K(VGS−VT)2, K = 1 mA/V², with no channel modulation. A small AC input drives the gate; a large coupling capacitor connects a 2 kilohm drain load. Find bias, validate saturation, then find gm and midband voltage gain.

提示Hint
直流负载电阻被耦合电容隔离；交流RD与RL并联。The coupling capacitor isolates RL for DC; RD and RL are parallel for AC.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. VOV=2V，IDQ=1(2)2=4mA。VOV=2V and IDQ=4mA.
  2. VDSQ=12−(4mA)(2k)=4V≥2V，恒流区成立。VDSQ=12−(4mA)(2k)=4V≥2V, confirming saturation.
  3. gm=2KVOV=4mS，Av=−gm(2k∥2k)=−4。gm=4mS and Av=−gm(2k∥2k)=−4.

IDQ = 4 mA，VDSQ = 4 V，gm = 4 mS，Av = −4。IDQ = 4 mA, VDSQ = 4 V, gm = 4 mS, Av = −4.

漏极直流KVL为8+4 = 12 V；小信号电流增量乘1 kΩ得到反相增益。评分：Q点4分，工作区2分，gm2分，增益2分。DC KVL: 8+4 = 12 V; incremental current through 1 kilohm gives the negative gain. Rubric: bias 4, region 2, gm 2, gain 2.

7. BJT偏置的精确加载7. BJT bias with base loading

10分10 points

NPN共射电路VCC = 12 V，RC = 2 kΩ，RE = 1 kΩ。基极偏置网络戴维南等效为2.7 V串联20 kΩ。取β = 99，VBE = 0.7 V，忽略Early效应。求IB、IC、IE、VC、VE、VCE，并验证放大区。An NPN common-emitter circuit has VCC = 12 V, RC = 2 kilohms and RE = 1 kilohm. Base bias is Thevenin-equivalent to 2.7 V in series with 20 kilohms. Use beta = 99, VBE = 0.7 V and no Early effect. Find IB, IC, IE, VC, VE and VCE, then validate forward-active operation.

提示Hint
不要把基极电流当0；发射极电流是(β+1)IB。Do not neglect base current; IE = (beta+1)IB.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. IB=(2.7−0.7)/[20k+100(1k)]=16.6667μA。IB=(2.7−0.7)/[20k+100(1k)]=16.6667μA.
  2. IC=1.65mA，IE=1.66667mA。IC=1.65mA and IE=1.66667mA.
  3. VE=1.66667V，VC=12−1.65(2)=8.7V，VCE=7.03333V。VE=1.66667V, VC=8.7V and VCE=7.03333V.
  4. VB=VE+0.7=2.36667V，VC>VB；BE正偏且BC反偏，放大区成立。VB=2.36667V and VC>VB; BE is forward biased and BC reverse biased.

IB = 16.667 μA，IC = 1.65 mA，IE = 1.6667 mA；VC = 8.7 V，VE = 1.6667 V，VCE = 7.0333 V。IB = 16.667 microamps, IC = 1.65 mA, IE = 1.6667 mA; VC = 8.7 V, VE = 1.6667 V and VCE = 7.0333 V.

基极回路0.3333+0.7+1.6667 = 2.7 V。评分：基极方程3分，电流2分，节点电压3分，工作区2分。Base-loop check: 0.3333+0.7+1.6667 = 2.7 V. Rubric: base equation 3, currents 2, voltages 3, region 2.

8. 有限增益与深负反馈近似8. Finite gain and deep-feedback approximation

10分10 points

采用负反馈约定xi = xid + xf，xf = Fxo，xo = Axid。A = 1000，F = 0.09，均为无量纲且频率无关。求精确闭环增益、深负反馈近似，并以精确值为基准算近似相对误差。Use negative-feedback convention xi = xid + xf, xf = Fxo and xo = Axid. Let A = 1000 and F = 0.09, dimensionless and frequency-independent. Find exact closed-loop gain, deep-feedback estimate and relative error referenced to the exact value.

提示Hint
先由三个关系消去xid和xf，再比较1/F。Eliminate xid and xf, then compare with 1/F.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. xo=A(xi−Fxo)，所以Af=A/(1+AF)=1000/91≈10.98901。xo=A(xi−Fxo) gives Af=1000/91≈10.98901.
  2. AF=90，近似Af≈1/F=100/9≈11.11111。AF=90, so the approximation is 1/F=100/9≈11.11111.
  3. 相对误差[(100/9)−(1000/91)]/(1000/91)=1/90≈1.1111%。The relative error is 1/90≈1.1111%.

精确10.9890；近似11.1111；相对误差1.1111%。Exact 10.9890; estimate 11.1111; relative error 1.1111%.

将Af乘(1+AF)应回到A；别把1.11%写成0.0111%。评分：关系推导3分，精确3分，近似2分，误差2分。Af(1+AF) must recover A. Distinguish a fraction 0.01111 from 1.111%. Rubric: derivation 3, exact 3, estimate 2, error 2.

9. 一阶有源低通9. First-order active low-pass

10分10 points

已知理想有源低通传递函数H(s)=2/(1+sRC)，R = 10 kΩ，C = 10 nF。求截止频率、截止处幅值和相位。再说明两个隔离的一阶低通级与未缓冲的两个RC网络为何不能总用同一乘积。An ideal active low-pass has H(s)=2/(1+sRC), R = 10 kilohms and C = 10 nF. Find cutoff, magnitude and phase at cutoff. Explain why multiplying two isolated first-order stages is not generally equivalent to two unbuffered RC networks.

提示Hint
截止处ωRC = 1；独立级相乘需要不相互加载。At cutoff omega RC = 1; stage multiplication assumes no interstage loading.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. RC=10−4s，fc=1/(2πRC)≈1591.549Hz。RC=10−4s and fc≈1591.549Hz.
  2. H(jωc)=2/(1+j)=1−j，所以|H|=2、相位−45∘。H(jωc)=1−j, so magnitude is 2 and phase is −45∘.
  3. 隔离级可以相乘；未缓冲RC的下一级输入阻抗改变上一级，因此必须分析整体网络。Isolated stages may be multiplied. An unbuffered second RC stage loads the first, requiring analysis of the full network.

fc ≈ 1.592 kHz；|H| = 1.4142，相位−45°；级间加载需计入。fc about 1.592 kHz; |H| = 1.4142 and phase −45 degrees; include interstage loading.

相对通带2的幅度比为0.7071，而绝对幅度为1.4142。评分：fc3分，幅相4分，加载解释3分。Relative to passband 2, the ratio is 0.7071 while absolute magnitude is 1.4142. Rubric: fc 3, magnitude/phase 4, loading 3.

10. 迟滞比较器与概念边界10. Hysteresis comparator and concept checks

10分10 points

理想反相迟滞比较器：信号vi接反相端，同相端v+ = 0.2vo，输出电平±10 V。求上门限、下门限、回差；说明从vi = −3 V逐渐上升到3 V时如何翻转。最后判断：两个差分输入都增加同样的Δ属于共模还是差模？An ideal inverting Schmitt trigger has vi at the inverting input, v+ = 0.2vo and output levels ±10 V. Find upper/lower thresholds and hysteresis. Track vi increasing from −3 to +3 V. Finally, if both differential inputs increase by the same delta, is that common- or differential-mode?

提示Hint
分别由当前高输出和低输出算反馈门限；不要全程用虚短。Compute thresholds for each output state; do not apply a virtual short throughout.
答案、步骤与检查Answer, working & checks
  1. 高输出时v+=2V，输入上升越过+2 V时输出由+10翻到−10 V。With output high, v+=2V; increasing vi past +2 V switches +10 to −10 V.
  2. 低输出时v+=−2V，输入下降越过−2 V时输出由−10翻到+10 V。With output low, v+=−2V; decreasing vi past −2 V switches −10 to +10 V.
  3. 回差2−(−2)=4V。从−3 V上升时先高，越过+2 V后低。Hysteresis is 4V. Increasing from −3 V starts high and switches low past +2 V.
  4. 两个输入同增Δ使差值不变，属于共模变化；仅需概念判断。Equal input increments leave their difference unchanged: common-mode change, assessed conceptually.

上门限+2 V，下门限−2 V，回差4 V；上升越过+2 V翻低；同增量属于共模。Thresholds +2 and −2 V, width 4 V; rising past +2 V switches low; equal increments are common-mode.

门限之间输出依历史，等号处不指定唯一稳态。评分：两门限4分，回差2分，路径2分，共模2分。Between thresholds the output depends on history; equality does not specify a unique steady state. Rubric: thresholds 4, width 2, path 2, common-mode 2.
